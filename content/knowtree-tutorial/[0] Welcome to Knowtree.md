# Welcome to Knowtree

Knowtree is a self-directed learning system that combines a visual knowledge graph in the browser with a Socratic tutor in your terminal. You navigate a directed acyclic graph (DAG) of topics, unlock new nodes by mastering prerequisites, and learn through guided dialogue with Claude. This tutorial graph will walk you through every feature of the app — from the graph view and classroom to assessments, shortcuts, and the progression system that ties it all together.
