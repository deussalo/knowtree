# Assessment and Mastery

After covering all subconcepts in a topic, the tutor administers a short assessment to verify understanding. Questions are generated based on the subconcepts covered during the session, and the tutor scores your responses, providing feedback on each. A passing score marks the node as completed and unlocks downstream nodes in the graph. If you don't pass, you can review the material and retake — your best score is always preserved.
