# The Socratic Tutor

The tutor is a Claude-powered Socratic teaching assistant that runs in your terminal via the `/tutor` command in Claude Code. Rather than lecturing, it guides you through each topic by asking probing questions, presenting examples for you to analyze, and helping you discover principles on your own. Each session breaks a topic into subconcepts, tracks your progress through them, and writes a live classroom document that the browser webapp renders in real time.
