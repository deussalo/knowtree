# Rewriting History

Powerful but dangerous techniques for reshaping your commit history before sharing it. You will amend the most recent commit with `git commit --amend` to fix a typo in its message or add a forgotten file. You will use interactive rebase to edit a commit in the middle of a branch, squash a fixup commit into its target, and drop an accidental commit entirely. You will practice using `git reflog` to recover from a botched rewrite by finding and restoring the original commit. Finally, you will explain the critical rule: never rewrite history that has already been pushed to a shared branch, and what goes wrong when you do.
