# Scaling Laws (Chinchilla, Kaplan)

Scaling laws quantify how model performance improves predictably as you increase parameters, data, and compute. Kaplan et al. showed power-law relationships; Chinchilla showed that most models were undertrained relative to their size. These empirical laws guide the most expensive decisions in AI — how big to make a model and how much data to train it on.
