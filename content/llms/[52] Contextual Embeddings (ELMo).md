# Contextual Embeddings (ELMo)

Unlike static word embeddings where "bank" always has the same vector, contextual embeddings (pioneered by ELMo) produce different representations depending on surrounding context. This was the breakthrough that showed deep bidirectional models could capture polysemy and context-dependent meaning, directly paving the way for BERT and the pretrain-then-fine-tune paradigm.
