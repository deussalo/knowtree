# Bias-Variance Tradeoff

The bias-variance tradeoff formalises why models can fail in two opposite ways: too simple (high bias, underfitting) or too complex (high variance, overfitting). This tradeoff governs architecture choices, regularisation decisions, and dataset sizing — and the surprising discovery that very large language models can be undertrained rather than overtrained challenges classical intuitions about it.
