# Evaluation Metrics & Cross-Validation

Accuracy, precision, recall, F1, perplexity, BLEU, ROUGE — choosing the right metric determines what "good" means for a model. Cross-validation provides reliable estimates of generalisation performance. Understanding evaluation is essential for interpreting LLM benchmarks and knowing when reported results actually mean something.
