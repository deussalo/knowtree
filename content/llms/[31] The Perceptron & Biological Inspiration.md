# The Perceptron & Biological Inspiration

The perceptron — a single artificial neuron that computes a weighted sum of inputs and applies a threshold — was the starting point for neural networks. Understanding its capabilities and limitations (the XOR problem) explains why multiple layers and nonlinear activations are necessary, setting the stage for the deep networks that power modern LLMs.
