# The Transformer Architecture

The transformer, introduced in "Attention Is All You Need" (2017), replaced recurrence entirely with self-attention and feedforward layers, enabling massive parallelisation during training. Its architecture — stacked blocks of multi-head attention, layer normalisation, and feedforward networks with residual connections — is the blueprint for every modern LLM, from BERT to GPT to Claude.
