# LSTMs & GRUs — Gating Mechanisms

LSTMs and GRUs solve the vanishing gradient problem in RNNs by introducing gates that control information flow — deciding what to remember, what to forget, and what to output. These gating mechanisms were the state of the art for sequence modelling before attention, and the gating concept itself reappears in modern architectures including mixture-of-experts LLMs.
