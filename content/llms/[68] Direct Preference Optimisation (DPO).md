# Direct Preference Optimisation (DPO)

DPO simplifies RLHF by eliminating the separate reward model and PPO loop, instead directly optimising the language model on preference pairs. This makes alignment training more stable and computationally cheaper. Understanding DPO and its relationship to RLHF shows how the field is evolving toward simpler, more efficient alignment techniques.
