# Supervised Learning

Supervised learning trains models on labelled input-output pairs — the paradigm behind classification, regression, and the fine-tuning stage of language models. Understanding how a model learns a mapping from inputs to targets, and what can go wrong in that process, is foundational to understanding both classical ML and modern LLM training pipelines.
