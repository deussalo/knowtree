# Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)

RAG combines a language model with an external retrieval system, allowing the model to access up-to-date or domain-specific knowledge beyond its training data. The retriever finds relevant documents; the generator conditions on them to produce grounded responses. Understanding RAG explains how LLMs can be made more factual, current, and specialised without retraining.
