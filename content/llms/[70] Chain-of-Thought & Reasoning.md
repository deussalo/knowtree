# Chain-of-Thought & Reasoning

Chain-of-thought prompting elicits step-by-step reasoning from language models, dramatically improving performance on complex tasks like mathematics, logic, and multi-hop question answering. Understanding how and why explicit reasoning chains help — and the fundamental limitations of LLM reasoning — is critical for knowing what these models can and cannot reliably do.
