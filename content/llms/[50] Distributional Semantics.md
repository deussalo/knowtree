# Distributional Semantics

The distributional hypothesis — "you shall know a word by the company it keeps" — provides the theoretical foundation for why word embeddings work. Words that appear in similar contexts develop similar representations. This principle scales from individual words to entire sentences and documents, and is the core insight behind why language models trained on text alone can capture meaning.
