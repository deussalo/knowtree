# Stochastic Gradient Descent & Variants

Full-batch gradient descent is impractical for large datasets. SGD and its variants (Adam, AdaGrad, RMSProp) use mini-batches and adaptive learning rates to make training feasible at scale. Understanding momentum, adaptive step sizes, and the noise introduced by mini-batching explains how billion-parameter models actually get trained.
