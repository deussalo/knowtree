# Deep Learning — Synthesis

This synthesis node connects neural network fundamentals (perceptrons, backpropagation, regularisation) through architectural innovations (CNNs, RNNs, LSTMs, encoder-decoder) to the threshold of the transformer era. The goal is to understand the trajectory: each architecture solved specific problems while creating new limitations, driving the field toward the attention mechanism.
