# Pretraining Objectives & Data

The choice of pretraining objective (masked LM, autoregressive, denoising) and the composition of training data (web crawl, books, code, curated vs uncurated) fundamentally determine what a language model knows and how it behaves. Understanding these choices explains why models have different strengths, biases, and failure modes — the data is the curriculum.
