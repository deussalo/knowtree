# Fine-Tuning & Transfer Learning

Fine-tuning takes a pretrained model and continues training on a smaller task-specific dataset, adapting general knowledge to specific applications. Transfer learning — the principle that representations learned on one task help with others — is why pretraining works at all. Understanding full fine-tuning, adapter methods, and LoRA explains how a single base model becomes many specialised ones.
