# Sequence-to-Sequence Models

Seq2seq models map variable-length input sequences to variable-length output sequences — the architecture behind early machine translation, summarisation, and dialogue systems. Understanding the encoder-decoder paradigm and its bottleneck problem (compressing all information into a fixed-length vector) directly motivates why the attention mechanism was invented.
