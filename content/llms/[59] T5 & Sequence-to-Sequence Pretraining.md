# T5 & Sequence-to-Sequence Pretraining

T5 (Text-to-Text Transfer Transformer) frames every NLP task as a text-to-text problem using a full encoder-decoder transformer. By unifying classification, translation, summarisation, and question answering under a single format, T5 showed that architectural flexibility combined with scale produces strong general-purpose language understanding and generation.
