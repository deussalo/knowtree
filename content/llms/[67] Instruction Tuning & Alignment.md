# Instruction Tuning & Alignment

Instruction tuning fine-tunes a model on datasets of (instruction, response) pairs, teaching it to follow diverse prompts. Alignment is the broader goal of making models behave in ways that are helpful, honest, and harmless. Together they explain why models trained on the same base can behave very differently depending on their post-training — and what "alignment" actually means technically.
