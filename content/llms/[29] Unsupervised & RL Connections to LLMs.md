# Unsupervised & RL Connections to LLMs

Pretraining is unsupervised learning at scale; RLHF is reinforcement learning applied to text generation; clustering and dimensionality reduction help interpret what models learn internally. This node connects the ML paradigms to their specific roles in the LLM pipeline, showing why a broad ML foundation matters for understanding language models.
