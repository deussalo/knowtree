# Word Embeddings (Word2Vec, GloVe)

Word embeddings map discrete words to dense vectors in a continuous space where semantic relationships become geometric ones — "king" minus "man" plus "woman" approximates "queen." Word2Vec and GloVe demonstrated that useful representations of meaning can be learned from raw text at scale, establishing the principle that underpins all language model representations.
