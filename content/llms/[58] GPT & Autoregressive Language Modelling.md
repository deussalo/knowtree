# GPT & Autoregressive Language Modelling

GPT (Generative Pre-trained Transformer) uses a decoder-only architecture trained to predict the next token given all previous tokens. This autoregressive approach — simple, scalable, and naturally suited to text generation — turned out to scale better than expected, and the GPT lineage (GPT-2, GPT-3, GPT-4) demonstrated that scaling autoregressive models produces emergent capabilities.
