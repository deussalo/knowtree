# Efficiency — Quantisation, Distillation, Pruning

Running billion-parameter models is expensive. Quantisation reduces numerical precision (FP16, INT8, INT4), distillation trains smaller models to mimic larger ones, and pruning removes unnecessary parameters. These techniques make LLMs practical for deployment on consumer hardware and at scale, and understanding them explains the tradeoffs between capability, cost, and accessibility.
