# Open vs Closed Models & the LLM Ecosystem

The LLM landscape spans fully open models (Llama, Mistral), API-only services (GPT-4, Claude), and everything in between. Understanding the tradeoffs — transparency vs safety, innovation vs control, cost vs capability — and the ecosystem of fine-tuning platforms, inference providers, and open-source communities provides context for the practical and political landscape of modern AI.
