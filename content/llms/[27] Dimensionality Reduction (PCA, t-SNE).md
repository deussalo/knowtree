# Dimensionality Reduction (PCA, t-SNE)

High-dimensional data (like word embeddings with hundreds of dimensions) is hard to visualise and expensive to process. PCA finds the directions of maximum variance to compress data linearly; t-SNE preserves local structure for visualisation. These tools are essential for understanding and debugging what embedding spaces actually look like.
