# Activation Functions

Activation functions (sigmoid, tanh, ReLU, GELU) introduce nonlinearity into neural networks, enabling them to learn complex patterns beyond what linear models can capture. The choice of activation function affects training dynamics, gradient flow, and model expressiveness — GELU specifically is the activation function used in most transformer-based language models.
