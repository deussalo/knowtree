# Norms, Distances & Similarity Measures

Norms quantify the size of vectors, distances measure how far apart two points are, and similarity measures like cosine similarity capture how aligned two directions are. These concepts appear everywhere in ML — from loss functions to nearest-neighbour retrieval to measuring how similar two word embeddings are.
