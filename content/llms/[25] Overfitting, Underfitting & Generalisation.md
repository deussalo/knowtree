# Overfitting, Underfitting & Generalisation

A model that memorises training data but fails on new data has overfit; one that captures no patterns has underfit. Generalisation — performing well on unseen data — is the ultimate goal. Understanding this dynamic explains why LLMs need massive and diverse training data, why scaling helps, and why evaluation on held-out benchmarks matters.
