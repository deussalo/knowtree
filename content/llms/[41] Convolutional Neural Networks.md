# Convolutional Neural Networks

CNNs use learned filters that slide across input data to detect local patterns — edges in images, n-grams in text. While transformers have largely replaced CNNs for language tasks, understanding convolutions provides essential context for why sequence models evolved as they did and how spatial pattern detection works in multimodal models that process both images and text.
