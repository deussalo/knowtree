# Safety, Alignment & Red-Teaming

Safety research addresses the risks of LLMs: generating harmful content, hallucination, jailbreaks, bias amplification, and potential misuse. Red-teaming systematically probes models for failure modes. Understanding the safety landscape — technical mitigations, governance frameworks, and open problems — is essential for anyone building with or reasoning about the societal impact of language models.
