# Recurrent Neural Networks

RNNs process sequences by maintaining a hidden state that gets updated at each time step, giving them a form of memory. They were the dominant architecture for language tasks before transformers, and understanding their strengths (sequential processing) and weaknesses (difficulty with long-range dependencies) explains exactly what problem the attention mechanism was designed to solve.
