# Conditional Probability & Bayes' Theorem

Language models predict the next token conditioned on all previous tokens — conditional probability is their core operation. Bayes' theorem formalises how to update beliefs given new evidence, a principle that underlies everything from naive Bayes classifiers to the posterior distributions used in Bayesian deep learning.
