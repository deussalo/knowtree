# Transformers & Language Modelling — Synthesis

This synthesis node connects embeddings, tokenisation, attention, transformer architecture, pretraining paradigms, scaling laws, and decoding strategies into a complete picture of how language models work from input text to generated output. The goal is to understand the full pipeline before examining how raw language models are turned into useful, aligned assistants.
