# Dropout & Regularisation in Deep Networks

Dropout randomly zeros out neurons during training, forcing the network to learn redundant representations that generalise better. Combined with weight decay, early stopping, and data augmentation, these regularisation techniques prevent deep networks from memorising training data. Understanding when and why to apply each technique is essential for training models that perform well on new data.
