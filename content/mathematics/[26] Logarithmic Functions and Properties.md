# Logarithmic Functions & Properties

Logarithms as the inverse of exponentiation — log rules (product, quotient, power), change of base formula, and natural logarithms (ln). Understanding logs lets you solve exponential equations, analyze algorithmic complexity (O(log n) means something specific), and work with multiplicative scales. The three bases that matter most: 2 (computing), e (calculus), 10 (engineering).
