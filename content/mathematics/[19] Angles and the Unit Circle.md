# Angles & the Unit Circle

Measuring angles in degrees and radians, the unit circle as a framework for understanding trigonometric functions, and the relationship between circular motion and right triangles. The unit circle provides the foundation for all trig — every sine and cosine value maps to a point on this circle, and understanding it makes the rest of trigonometry click into place.
