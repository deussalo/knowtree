# Sequences, Series & Sigma Notation

Ordered lists of numbers following a pattern (arithmetic and geometric sequences), the sums of those sequences (series), and the compact notation (Σ) for expressing sums. Geometric series describe signal decay and filter responses in electronics. Sigma notation is used throughout higher mathematics and appears directly in programming as loop-based accumulation.
