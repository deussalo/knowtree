# Exponential Functions & Growth/Decay

Functions of the form f(x) = a·bˣ and the natural exponential e^x. Exponential growth models unconstrained doubling (Moore's law, bacterial growth), while exponential decay describes radioactive decay, capacitor discharge, and signal attenuation. The number e ≈ 2.718 appears naturally whenever growth rate is proportional to current value.
