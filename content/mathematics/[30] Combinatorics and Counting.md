# Combinatorics & Counting

Systematic methods for counting arrangements and selections — permutations (order matters), combinations (order doesn't), and the multiplication principle. Combinatorics answers questions like "how many possible passwords exist?" or "how many ways can signals be routed?" It's the mathematical foundation for probability and appears in algorithm analysis and cryptography.
