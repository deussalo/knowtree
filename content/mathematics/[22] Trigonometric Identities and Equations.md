# Trigonometric Identities & Equations

Key identities (Pythagorean, double-angle, sum/difference) and techniques for solving equations involving trig functions. Identities let you simplify complex trigonometric expressions — essential when analyzing circuits, simplifying integrals, or transforming signals. Solving trig equations is needed for finding angles, frequencies, and phase shifts.
