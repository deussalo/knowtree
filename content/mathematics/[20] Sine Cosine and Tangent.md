# Sine, Cosine & Tangent

The three primary trigonometric functions — what they measure, how to compute them from right triangles (SOH-CAH-TOA), their graphs as periodic waves, and their key values at standard angles. These functions describe oscillation, rotation, and wave behavior. In electronics, voltage and current in AC circuits are modeled as sinusoids.
