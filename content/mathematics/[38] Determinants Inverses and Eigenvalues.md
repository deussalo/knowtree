# Determinants, Inverses & Eigenvalues

The determinant tells you if a matrix is invertible and how it scales area/volume. Matrix inverses let you solve Ax = b as x = A⁻¹b. Eigenvalues and eigenvectors reveal the fundamental modes of a linear system — the directions that don't change under transformation. These concepts are critical for stability analysis, principal component analysis, and vibration/resonance in circuits.
