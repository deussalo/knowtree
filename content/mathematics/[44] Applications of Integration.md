# Applications of Integration

Using integrals to compute areas, volumes, average values, work, and energy. In electronics, integration calculates energy stored in capacitors and inductors, RMS values of AC signals, and total charge flow. In programming, numerical integration methods (trapezoidal rule, Simpson's rule) let you approximate integrals computationally when analytical solutions are impractical.
