# Rearranging Formulas

Taking a formula like V = IR and solving for any variable (I = V/R, R = V/I). This skill — isolating a target variable by performing inverse operations — is used constantly in physics, electronics, and engineering. It requires understanding the order of operations in reverse and treating multi-variable equations as puzzles to untangle.
