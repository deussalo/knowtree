# Derivatives & Differentiation Rules

The derivative measures instantaneous rate of change — the slope of a function at a point. Rules for differentiation (power rule, product rule, quotient rule, chain rule) let you find derivatives without computing limits every time. Derivatives tell you velocity from position, current from charge, and gradient direction for optimization algorithms.
