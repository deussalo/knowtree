# Integration & the Fundamental Theorem

Integration as the reverse of differentiation and as accumulation of area under a curve. The Fundamental Theorem of Calculus connects these two views. Integration techniques (substitution, parts, partial fractions) are tools for computing integrals. In electronics, integrating current over time gives charge; in programming, numerical integration approximates areas and volumes.
