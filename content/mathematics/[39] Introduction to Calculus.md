# Introduction to Calculus

The mathematics of change and accumulation. Calculus provides tools to analyze how quantities change over time (derivatives) and how to accumulate quantities (integrals). For electronics, calculus describes how capacitors and inductors behave. For programming, it underpins optimization, machine learning, physics simulations, and signal processing. This section builds from limits through differential equations.
