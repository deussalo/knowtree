# Algebra Fundamentals

The language of mathematics. Algebra lets you describe relationships between quantities using variables and equations, solve for unknowns, and express general patterns. Every formula in electronics (Ohm's law, Kirchhoff's laws) and every algorithm in programming relies on algebraic thinking. This section takes you from basic expressions through functions and systems of equations.
