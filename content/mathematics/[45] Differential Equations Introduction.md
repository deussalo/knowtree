# Differential Equations Introduction

Equations involving functions and their derivatives — they describe how systems evolve over time. A capacitor charging, a spring oscillating, population dynamics, and heat flow are all modeled by differential equations. This topic covers what DEs are, their classification (order, linearity), and the concept of a solution as a function (not a number).
