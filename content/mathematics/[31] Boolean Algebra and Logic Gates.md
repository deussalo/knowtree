# Boolean Algebra & Logic Gates

The algebra of true/false values — AND, OR, NOT, XOR, NAND, NOR operations and their laws (De Morgan's, associativity, distributivity, absorption). Boolean algebra maps directly to digital logic gates, which are the physical building blocks of all digital circuits. Every if-statement in code and every transistor arrangement in a chip implements boolean logic.
