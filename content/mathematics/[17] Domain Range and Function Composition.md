# Domain, Range & Function Composition

Understanding what inputs a function accepts (domain), what outputs it produces (range), and how to chain functions together (composition: f(g(x))). These concepts are critical for understanding valid inputs to mathematical operations, avoiding errors like division by zero or square roots of negatives, and for modeling pipelines where one operation feeds into another.
