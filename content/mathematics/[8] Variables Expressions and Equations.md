# Variables, Expressions & Equations

Using letters to represent unknown or changing quantities, building algebraic expressions, and solving basic equations. This covers translating word problems into equations, combining like terms, and the fundamental principle that whatever you do to one side of an equation, you must do to the other. The starting point for all algebraic manipulation.
