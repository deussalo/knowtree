# Functions & Graphing

The concept of a function as a machine that takes an input and produces an output — f(x) notation, evaluating functions, and plotting them on a coordinate plane. Functions are the mathematical equivalent of programming functions: they map inputs to outputs. Understanding their graphical behavior (shape, intercepts, behavior) builds intuition for everything from signal analysis to algorithm design.
