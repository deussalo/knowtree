# Logarithmic Scales & Applications

Using logarithmic scales to visualize and reason about quantities that span many orders of magnitude. Decibels (dB) in electronics measure power and voltage ratios logarithmically. Bode plots use log-frequency axes to visualize circuit frequency response. Log-log and semi-log plots reveal power-law and exponential relationships that are invisible on linear scales.
