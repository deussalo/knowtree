# Polar Coordinates & Phasors

Representing points by angle and distance from origin instead of x-y coordinates, and using phasors (rotating vectors) to represent sinusoidal signals. Polar form simplifies multiplication and division of complex quantities, and phasor analysis is the standard method for analyzing AC circuits — converting differential equations into simple algebra.
