# Limits & Continuity

What happens to a function's output as its input approaches a particular value. Limits formalize the idea of "getting infinitely close" and are the rigorous foundation for derivatives and integrals. Continuity — a function with no breaks, jumps, or holes — determines where calculus operations are valid. Understanding limits builds the conceptual bridge from algebra to calculus.
