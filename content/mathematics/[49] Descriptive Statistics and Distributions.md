# Descriptive Statistics & Distributions

Summarizing data with measures of center (mean, median, mode) and spread (variance, standard deviation), and understanding probability distributions (uniform, normal/Gaussian, binomial). The normal distribution describes noise in electronic measurements and natural variation in data. Standard deviation quantifies signal quality (SNR), and distributions underpin hypothesis testing and data analysis in programming.
