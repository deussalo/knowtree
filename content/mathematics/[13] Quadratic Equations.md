# Quadratic Equations

Equations of the form ax² + bx + c = 0 — solving them by factoring, completing the square, and the quadratic formula. Quadratics model parabolic trajectories, appear in optimization problems, and their discriminant tells you about the nature of solutions. Understanding quadratics is prerequisite to working with higher-order polynomials and many physics/electronics applications.
