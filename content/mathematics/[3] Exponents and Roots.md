# Exponents & Roots

Powers, square roots, cube roots, and the laws of exponents (product rule, quotient rule, power of a power, negative and fractional exponents). Exponents are fundamental to understanding growth, scientific notation, logarithms, and how computers represent numbers. Root operations are the inverse of powers and appear throughout algebra and electronics.
