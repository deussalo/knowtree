# Exponentials & Logarithms

Two sides of the same coin — exponential functions model growth and decay (charging capacitors, population growth, compound interest), while logarithms undo exponentiation and compress wide-ranging scales into manageable ones (decibels, pH, algorithmic complexity). Together they are among the most practically important functions in applied mathematics.
