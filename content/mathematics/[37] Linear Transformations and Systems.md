# Linear Transformations & Systems

Viewing matrices as functions that transform space — rotation, reflection, scaling, shearing — and using matrix equations (Ax = b) to solve systems of linear equations. Gaussian elimination and row reduction provide systematic solution methods. This unifies the algebraic approach (systems of equations) with the geometric view (transforming vectors).
