# First-Order Differential Equations

Solving DEs with only first derivatives — separable equations, integrating factors, and the exponential solution to dy/dt = ky. First-order DEs model RC circuits (capacitor charging/discharging), RL circuits, thermal cooling, and any system where the rate of change depends on the current state. These are the most commonly encountered DEs in practical engineering.
