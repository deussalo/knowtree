# Second-Order Differential Equations & Circuits

DEs with second derivatives — they describe systems with inertia and restoring forces. RLC circuits (resistor-inductor-capacitor) are governed by second-order DEs, producing oscillatory, overdamped, or critically damped responses. The characteristic equation, natural frequency, and damping ratio determine system behavior. This is where phasor analysis and calculus converge for circuit analysis.
