# The First Fleet & Founding of Sydney (1788)

Eleven ships carrying roughly 1,400 people — more than half of them convicts — arrived at Botany Bay in January 1788 and soon relocated to Sydney Cove. Governor Arthur Phillip's commission, the struggle to establish food supply, the first interactions with the Eora people, and the devastating smallpox epidemic of 1789 defined the colony's earliest years. The letters and dispatches Phillip sent back to London, alongside accounts from officers like Watkin Tench, provide firsthand testimony of both colonial ambition and immediate crisis.
