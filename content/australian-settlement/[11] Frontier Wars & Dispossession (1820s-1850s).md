# Frontier Wars & Dispossession (1820s-1850s)

As pastoral expansion accelerated, so did organised violence against Aboriginal peoples. The Bathurst Wars, the Myall Creek massacre of 1838 and the unprecedented trial that followed, the Black War in Van Diemen's Land, and systematic dispossession across Queensland and Victoria constituted an undeclared war along a moving frontier. This node draws on court records, settler diaries, missionary accounts, and surviving oral histories to examine the scale, patterns, and consequences of frontier violence — and why it was largely erased from mainstream Australian memory for over a century.
