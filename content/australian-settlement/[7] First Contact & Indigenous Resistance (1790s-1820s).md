# First Contact & Indigenous Resistance (1790s-1820s)

From Pemulwuy's guerrilla campaign against the Sydney settlement in the 1790s to Windradyne's resistance in the Bathurst region in the 1820s, Aboriginal peoples actively opposed colonial encroachment from the very beginning. These were not isolated skirmishes but sustained strategic responses to invasion. Combining the knowledge of pre-contact Indigenous society with the realities of early colonial expansion, this node examines how first contact unfolded as a military, biological, and cultural confrontation — and how colonial records systematically minimised or reframed Indigenous agency.
