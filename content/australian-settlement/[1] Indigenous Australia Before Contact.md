# Indigenous Australia Before Contact

The world's oldest continuous civilisation occupied every ecological zone of the Australian continent for at least 60,000 years before any European arrived. Understanding the depth, diversity, and sophistication of Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander societies is essential context for everything that follows — without it, the European arrival appears to happen on an empty stage, which is the foundational myth that colonisers used to justify dispossession.
