# The Gold Rush (1851-1860s)

The discovery of gold near Bathurst in 1851 and the massive Ballarat and Bendigo fields that followed triggered the most dramatic transformation in Australian colonial history. Population doubled in a decade, Melbourne became one of the world's richest cities, and the social order was upended as labourers could suddenly become wealthy. The gold rush also drew immigrants from across the globe — particularly China — creating new tensions around race, labour, and who belonged in the colonies. Gold licence fees and the heavy-handed enforcement that accompanied them sowed the seeds of democratic revolt.
