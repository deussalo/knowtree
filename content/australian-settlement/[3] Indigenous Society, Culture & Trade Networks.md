# Indigenous Society, Culture & Trade Networks

Far from the "primitive nomads" of colonial imagination, Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples maintained complex kinship systems, land management practices including fire-stick farming, extensive trade networks spanning thousands of kilometres, and legal and spiritual frameworks encoded in oral tradition. Understanding these systems — particularly custodial relationships with Country — is necessary to grasp why European notions of terra nullius and private land ownership represented not just a legal fiction but a fundamental collision of worldviews.
