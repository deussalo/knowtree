# Early European Exploration (1606-1770)

Dutch, Portuguese, and other European mariners encountered the Australian coastline more than 160 years before the British claimed it. Willem Janszoon's 1606 landing, Dirk Hartog's 1616 plate, Abel Tasman's 1642 circumnavigation, and William Dampier's 1688 visit each left records but no permanent settlement. Examining why these earlier contacts did not lead to colonisation reveals how strategic priorities, geographic knowledge, and imperial ambition shaped which power ultimately claimed the continent.
