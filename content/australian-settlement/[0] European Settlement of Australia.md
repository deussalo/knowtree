# European Settlement of Australia

An overview of the forces that brought Europeans to the Australian continent and the consequences that followed for both settlers and the Indigenous peoples who had inhabited the land for over 60,000 years. This course traces the arc from first Dutch sightings through British colonisation, convict transportation, pastoral expansion, gold rushes, and the emergence of distinct colonial identities, ending at the threshold of Federation in the 1890s. Primary sources — letters, proclamations, parliamentary records, and firsthand accounts — anchor each topic in the voices of those who lived it.
