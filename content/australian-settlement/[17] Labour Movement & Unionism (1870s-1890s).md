# Labour Movement & Unionism (1870s-1890s)

The 1890s maritime strike and the great shearers' strikes that followed were defining moments for Australian labour and politics. The eight-hour day movement, the formation of trade unions, and the creation of the Australian Labor Party in 1891 — one of the world's first labour parties — emerged from the collision between workers' demands and employers backed by colonial governments willing to use military force. Strike manifestos, union minute books, and the Bulletin magazine's commentary provide firsthand accounts of how industrial conflict forged a political movement that would shape the nation.
