# Oscillators & Waveforms

An oscillator is a circuit or algorithm that generates a periodic waveform. This topic covers the four fundamental waveform shapes used in synthesis — sine (pure tone), square (hollow, clarinet-like), sawtooth (bright, buzzy), and triangle (soft, flute-like) — along with their harmonic content and how each is generated. You'll understand why these particular shapes are useful starting points and how their spectral characteristics map to the Fourier series decompositions you've studied.
