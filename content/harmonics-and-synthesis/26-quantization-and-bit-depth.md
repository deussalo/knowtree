# Quantization & Bit Depth

Each digital audio sample must be rounded to a finite number of discrete amplitude levels, a process called quantization. This topic covers how bit depth determines the number of available levels (16-bit = 65,536 levels), the relationship between bit depth and dynamic range (approximately 6 dB per bit), quantization noise as the error introduced by rounding, and dithering as a technique to convert harsh quantization distortion into benign low-level noise.
