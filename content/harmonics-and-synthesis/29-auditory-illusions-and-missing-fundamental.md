# Auditory Illusions & Missing Fundamental

The auditory system actively constructs perception, sometimes "hearing" things that aren't physically present. This topic covers the missing fundamental phenomenon (hearing a pitch corresponding to a fundamental frequency that has been removed, because the brain infers it from the remaining harmonics), beating (the amplitude pulsation when two close frequencies interfere), combination tones (phantom frequencies generated by nonlinear ear response), and Shepard tones (the auditory barber pole — an endlessly ascending pitch).
