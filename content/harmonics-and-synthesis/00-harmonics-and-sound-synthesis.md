# Harmonics & Sound Synthesis — Overview

This graph explores the mathematics and physics behind musical sound, from the basic anatomy of waves through harmonic series, Fourier analysis, and practical synthesis techniques. You'll learn how pure sine waves combine to create complex tones, how digital systems capture and reproduce sound, and how human perception shapes what we hear as music. The journey bridges pure mathematics (trigonometry, complex exponentials, Fourier theory) with hands-on synthesis methods (additive, subtractive, FM) and the psychoacoustic phenomena that make it all musical.
