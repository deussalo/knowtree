# Harmonics & Overtones

When a string, air column, or membrane vibrates, it produces not just a fundamental frequency f but a series of overtones at integer multiples: 2f, 3f, 4f, and so on. These are the harmonics, and their relative strengths define the timbre of a sound. This topic covers the harmonic series, why integer-multiple frequencies arise from the physics of standing waves, how different instruments produce different harmonic spectra, and why the harmonic series is the natural basis for musical consonance and Fourier analysis.
