# Notes, Pitch & Frequency

Musical pitch is our perception of a sound's fundamental frequency. This topic covers how specific frequencies map to named notes (A4 = 440 Hz by convention), how octaves correspond to frequency doublings, and how the equal temperament system divides the octave into 12 semitones with a constant frequency ratio of 2^(1/12) between adjacent notes. You'll also see how this modern tuning system differs from the pure integer ratios of just intonation.
