# Digital-to-Analog Conversion

Converting digital samples back into a continuous analog signal requires reconstruction. This topic covers how a DAC (digital-to-analog converter) works, the role of the reconstruction filter (smoothing the staircase output), anti-aliasing filters on the input side, and why the Nyquist theorem guarantees perfect reconstruction when the sampling conditions are met. Understanding DAC operation connects the mathematical theory of sampling to the physical reality of hearing sound from speakers.
