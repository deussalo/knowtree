# FM Synthesis

Frequency modulation synthesis uses one oscillator (the modulator) to rapidly vary the frequency of another (the carrier), generating complex spectra from just two sine waves. This topic covers the carrier-modulator relationship, how the modulation index controls spectral richness, the mathematical prediction of sideband frequencies and amplitudes using Bessel functions, and why FM synthesis can efficiently produce metallic, bell-like, and evolving timbres that are difficult to achieve with additive or subtractive methods.
