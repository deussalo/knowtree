# Sine Waves

The sine wave is the simplest possible sound — a single frequency with no overtones, described by y(t) = A sin(2*pi*f*t + phi). This topic covers why sine waves are the fundamental building blocks of all sound, how amplitude A, frequency f, and phase phi each affect what you hear, and why Fourier showed that every periodic waveform can be decomposed into a sum of sine waves. A pure sine tone is what you hear from a tuning fork or a test signal generator.
