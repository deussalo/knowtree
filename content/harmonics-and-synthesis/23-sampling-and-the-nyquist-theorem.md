# Sampling & the Nyquist Theorem

To represent a continuous waveform digitally, we take discrete samples at regular intervals. This topic covers the sampling process, the Nyquist-Shannon theorem (a signal can be perfectly reconstructed if sampled at more than twice its highest frequency), aliasing (what happens when you violate the Nyquist limit), and why standard audio uses 44,100 Hz or 48,000 Hz sample rates. Understanding sampling is essential for working with any digital audio system.
