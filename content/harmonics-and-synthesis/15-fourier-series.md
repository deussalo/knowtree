# Fourier Series

Any periodic function can be written as a sum of sines and cosines at integer-multiple frequencies. This topic covers how to compute Fourier coefficients using integration, how truncating the series produces approximations with the Gibbs phenomenon at discontinuities, and classic examples: the square wave (odd harmonics with 1/n amplitudes), sawtooth wave (all harmonics with 1/n amplitudes), and triangle wave (odd harmonics with 1/n^2 amplitudes). The Fourier series is the mathematical formalization of the harmonic decomposition you encountered qualitatively.
