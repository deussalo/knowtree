# Additive Synthesis

Additive synthesis builds complex sounds from scratch by summing individual sine wave oscillators, each with independent control over frequency, amplitude, and phase. This topic covers how to reconstruct any timbre by specifying its harmonic spectrum, practical techniques like Hammond organ drawbars and spectral modeling, the computational cost (one oscillator per partial), and the relationship to Fourier series — additive synthesis is literally the Fourier series made audible.
