# Complex Tones

Real musical sounds are never pure sine waves — they are complex tones built from multiple frequency components superimposed together. This topic covers how a complex periodic waveform can be understood as a sum of sinusoids at integer-multiple frequencies, why different instruments playing the same note sound different (the relative amplitudes and phases of their components differ), and how this decomposition connects the physics of vibration to the perception of timbre.
