# Anatomy of a Wave

Every sound begins as a vibration propagating through a medium. This topic covers the fundamental parameters that describe any wave: frequency (how many cycles per second, measured in Hertz), amplitude (the peak displacement, related to loudness), period (the duration of one cycle), wavelength (the spatial extent of one cycle), and how these quantities relate to each other. Understanding these parameters is essential for describing and manipulating any sound.
