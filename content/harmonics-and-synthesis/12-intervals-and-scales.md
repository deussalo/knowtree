# Intervals & Scales

Musical intervals are defined by the frequency ratio between two notes. This topic covers how simple ratios like 3:2 (perfect fifth) and 4:3 (perfect fourth) arise naturally from the harmonic series, how scales are constructed by stacking intervals, and the fundamental tension between just intonation (pure ratios, key-dependent) and equal temperament (slightly impure ratios, freedom to modulate). Understanding intervals mathematically reveals why certain note combinations sound harmonious.
