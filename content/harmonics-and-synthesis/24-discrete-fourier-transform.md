# Discrete Fourier Transform

The DFT adapts the continuous Fourier transform for sampled digital signals. This topic covers the DFT formula, how it maps N time-domain samples to N frequency bins, the relationship between bin index and physical frequency, spectral leakage and windowing, and the computational breakthrough of the Fast Fourier Transform (FFT) algorithm that reduces the cost from O(N^2) to O(N log N). The DFT is the workhorse of digital audio analysis and processing.
