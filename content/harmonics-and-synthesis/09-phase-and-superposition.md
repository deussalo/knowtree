# Phase & Superposition

When two or more waves occupy the same space, they combine through superposition — their amplitudes add point by point. This topic covers the principle of superposition, how phase relationships between waves determine whether they reinforce (constructive interference) or cancel (destructive interference), and how these ideas extend to combining any number of waves. Phase is the key to understanding why the same set of frequency components can produce very different waveforms depending on their relative timing.
