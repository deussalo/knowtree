# Envelopes & Amplitude Shaping

An envelope controls how a sound's characteristics evolve over time. This topic covers the ADSR model (Attack, Decay, Sustain, Release), how envelope shape affects the perception of a sound's character (a slow attack sounds like a pad, a fast attack like a pluck), applying envelopes to parameters beyond amplitude (filter cutoff, pitch, modulation depth), and why temporal evolution is as important as spectral content in defining a sound's identity.
