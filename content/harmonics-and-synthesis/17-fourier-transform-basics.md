# Fourier Transform Basics

While the Fourier series handles periodic signals, the Fourier transform extends this decomposition to arbitrary signals by letting the period go to infinity. This topic covers the forward transform (time to frequency), the inverse transform (frequency to time), the concept of a frequency spectrum, and key properties like linearity, time-shifting, and the convolution theorem. The Fourier transform is the bridge between time-domain waveforms and frequency-domain spectra.
