# Trigonometric Foundations

Trigonometric functions are the mathematical language of periodic phenomena. This topic covers the sine and cosine functions as circular projections, angular frequency omega = 2*pi*f, key identities for combining waves (sum-to-product, product-to-sum), and how any sinusoidal wave A sin(omega*t + phi) can be decomposed into a sum of pure sine and cosine terms. These identities are the tools you'll use throughout Fourier analysis and synthesis.
