# Synthesis Techniques

This section covers the practical methods used to generate and shape sounds electronically. You'll explore oscillators that produce basic waveforms, then learn how additive synthesis builds complex tones from sine components, subtractive synthesis carves sounds by filtering harmonics, and FM synthesis creates rich timbres through frequency modulation. Envelope shaping ties it all together by controlling how sounds evolve over time.
