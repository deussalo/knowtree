# Perception of Pitch & Consonance

Our perception of pitch and harmony is shaped by both physics and neuroscience. This topic covers how the basilar membrane in the cochlea performs a crude frequency analysis, why intervals with simple frequency ratios (octave 2:1, fifth 3:2) sound consonant — their harmonics align rather than clash, the concept of critical bandwidth and sensory dissonance, and how cultural exposure interacts with these physical foundations.
