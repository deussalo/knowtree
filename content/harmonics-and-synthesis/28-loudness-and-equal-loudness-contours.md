# Loudness & Equal-Loudness Contours

Perceived loudness does not track physical sound intensity uniformly across frequencies. This topic covers the decibel scale (logarithmic measure of intensity ratios), Fletcher-Munson / ISO 226 equal-loudness contours showing that human hearing is most sensitive around 2-5 kHz and less sensitive at low and very high frequencies, the phon and sone scales for perceived loudness, and how these curves change with overall level. These curves directly influence how audio is mixed, compressed, and equalized.
