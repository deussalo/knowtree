# Mathematics of Waves

This section develops the mathematical tools for analyzing and constructing waveforms. Starting from trigonometric identities, you'll progress through Fourier series (decomposing periodic signals into harmonics), complex exponentials (Euler's formula and phasors), and the Fourier transform (moving between time and frequency domains). These tools are the theoretical backbone of all signal processing and synthesis.
