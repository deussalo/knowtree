# Complex Exponentials & Euler's Formula

Euler's formula e^(i*theta) = cos(theta) + i*sin(theta) unifies trigonometry and exponential functions in the complex plane. This topic covers how complex exponentials simplify the representation of sinusoidal waves, the concept of phasors (rotating vectors in the complex plane), and how the Fourier series becomes more elegant when written in complex form. The complex exponential notation is the standard language of signal processing and makes many derivations dramatically simpler.
